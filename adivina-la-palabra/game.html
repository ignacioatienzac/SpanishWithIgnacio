How to play

Guess the hidden Spanish word in six tries. Each guess must have the correct number of letters.

  • Type your guess using the on-screen keyboard or your own keyboard, then press ENTER to submit.
  • Tile colors show your progress: green is correct, yellow means the letter is in the word but in a different spot, and gray means it is not in the word.
  • Use the ⌫ key to delete letters before submitting.

Hints unlock after your third attempt. When the “Get a clue!” button becomes active, press it to reveal them.

Have fun and keep practicing your Spanish vocabulary!
