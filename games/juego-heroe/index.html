How to play

Pick your grammar path, choose a tense, select the verb type, and decide if you want to type answers or pick from options.

  • Write Mode: Type the correct conjugation and press Check. Use the number keys 1-5 to quickly add accented vowels (á, é, í, ó, ú).
  • Choice Mode: Select the correct conjugation from the grid to attack the monsters.
  • Protect the castle—each correct answer increases your attack power, while mistakes cost lives.

You can switch modes and difficulties from the start screen. Focus on accuracy to strengthen your hero!
